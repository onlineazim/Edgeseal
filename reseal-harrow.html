Keeping mould off a fresh seal

A new bead lasts a lot longer with a few small habits. None of these take more than a minute.

  • Run the extractor fan (or crack a window) during and for 15 minutes after a shower — trapped steam is the main cause of black mould.
  • Give the sealant line a quick squeegee or towel wipe after bathing, rather than letting water sit in the joint.
  • Rinse and wring out bath mats and shower curtains, and let them dry flat rather than bunched against the tub.
  • Wipe the seal weekly with a mild bathroom cleaner rather than reaching for bleach daily, which can dry out silicone early.
  • Don't caulk over an existing bead "to top it up" — trapped moisture underneath will still spread; it needs stripping back first.
  • If a small dark spot appears, treat it straight away — early mould wipes off, established mould has usually gone into the silicone itself.
